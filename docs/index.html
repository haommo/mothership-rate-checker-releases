Trucking Rate Checker

Trucking Rate Checker is an internal desktop application (macOS and Windows) used by our logistics team to check trucking freight rates for shipments and to keep a history of those checks.

What the app does

  • Imports shipment lists and checks trucking rates for each shipment.
  • Keeps a local history of rate checks on the user's computer.
  • Exports a packing list of selected checks to a Google Sheet. The sheet is created in the company's own Google Drive folder and shared by link, so the team can open it in a browser.

Google account access

The app uses a single company Google account, with the drive.file permission only. This lets the app create files in that account's Drive and manage only the files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in Google Drive. App users do not sign in with their own Google accounts.
